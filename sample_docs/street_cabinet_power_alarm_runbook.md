# Street Cabinet Power Alarm Runbook

> Synthetic sample document for the FieldDocs RAG demo. Not a real operator manual.

## Before opening the cabinet

Two people are required when working inside a powered street cabinet. Wear insulated gloves rated to 1000 V. Check for water ingress before touching any equipment, and do not open the cabinet in heavy rain.

## Battery alarm

The cabinet runs on mains power with a battery backup. A BATTERY LOW alarm means the backup batteries are below 46 volts. A healthy battery string reads between 52 and 54 volts on float charge.

Replace the battery string if any single battery reads below 11.5 volts after a full charge. Batteries should be replaced every 4 years even if they test good.

## Mains failure alarm

A MAINS FAIL alarm means the cabinet is running on battery. Batteries give about 4 hours of backup at normal load. Check the mains isolator and the supply fuse first. If the supply from the electricity network is down, log the fault with the power company reference number.

## High temperature alarm

The cabinet fan should start at 35 degrees Celsius. A HIGH TEMP alarm is raised at 55 degrees Celsius. Check that the fan runs and the air filter is clean. Replace the air filter every 12 months.
